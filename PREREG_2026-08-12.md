# Pre-registration — R19 seed replication + volume-matched pruning control

**Written 2026-08-12, before any of the three runs below existed.** Locked so
that the reported claim cannot be selected after seeing the numbers. Nothing in
this file may be edited once the first job starts; corrections go in a dated
appendix at the bottom.

## Why these two gaps

The manuscript states both in its own text:

- `sec/4_findings.tex:314` — "The arm is run on seed 0 only, so it inherits none
  of the three-seed replication the family gap has."
- `sec/4_findings.tex:318-324` — the veto removes 61.6% of the base's boxes, so
  "reduced supervision *volume* is not separated from misdirected supervision;
  a random-pruning control matched to the same removal rate would settle it and
  we did not run one."

These are the two named handles a referee has on the paper's co-headline result.

## Arms

| Tag | GPU | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `R19_ssl_5p_s1_perclass_and_consensus` | 3 | R19 keep rule, seed 1 |
| `R19_ssl_5p_s2_perclass_and_consensus` | 6 | R19 keep rule, seed 2 |
| `R20_ssl_5p_s0_perclass_random_prune` | 7 | volume-matched random pruning, seed 0 |

Everything except the pseudo-label `ann_file` is inherited from the same base as
every other arm: same initialization, same schedule, same CBGS wrapper, same 10
epochs, same `val_full=6019` external eval on the raw ep10 checkpoint.

### The pruning control, precisely

Starts from the **same confidence-gated candidate pool as R19** — the
`perclass_only` mask, not the raw dump. For each (keyframe, class) it removes
exactly as many boxes as the conjunctive rule removes from that same
(keyframe, class), choosing them uniformly at random. Matching per
(keyframe, class) implies matching per scene and per dump. Both masks are
computed in one pass inside `filter_perclass_random_prune`
(`tools/g2_make_pseudo_infos.py`), so the counts cannot drift from the arm they
are matched to; the subset property is asserted at runtime.

RNG: `numpy.default_rng(19)`, fixed via `--prune-seed 19`.

**Known limitation, pre-registered:** this is *one* draw. The bootstrap interval
below covers evaluation-set uncertainty, not draw-to-draw variance of the random
pruning itself. We will not claim a precise point value for the volume effect,
only the qualitative decomposition below.

## Statistics — fixed now

Paired scene-level block bootstrap, B=1000, 150 val scenes, identical protocol
and driver (`tools/g2_bootstrap_ci.py`) as every published interval in the paper.
Primary quantities:

1. `NDS(R19_s1) - NDS(R7b_s1)` and `NDS(R19_s2) - NDS(R7b_s2)` — the veto cost, per seed.
2. `NDS(R20_s0) - NDS(R7b_s0)` — the cost of removing the same volume at random.
3. `NDS(R19_s0) - NDS(R20_s0)` — the direction effect, i.e. what the camera veto
   costs *over and above* losing that many boxes.

Reference values already published: `R7b_s0 = 0.4150`, `R19_s0 = 0.3332`,
difference `-8.18` NDS, CI `[-9.63, -6.44]`.

## Decision rules — what each outcome licenses

### Seed replication (arms 1 and 2)

| Outcome | What the paper will say |
|---|---|
| **A1** both seeds negative, both CIs exclude zero | The veto cost replicates on three seeds. Limitation (vii)'s seed-0 caveat is removed; §4.4 and the abstract drop "on seed 0" for this result. |
| **A2** mixed — one seed negative with CI excluding zero, the other null or positive | The veto cost **does not replicate**. The seed-0 caveat stays and is strengthened to "the conjunctive result is seed-dependent". §5's "composing the gates does not rescue them" is downgraded to a seed-0 observation. The D2 family gap is untouched either way — it has its own three-seed replication. |
| **A3** both seeds null or positive | The seed-0 conjunctive result does not generalise. We retract "75% of the gap survives gate-matching" as a general claim and restate it as seed-0-specific, and the confidence-gate confound returns to open. This would be reported in the abstract, not buried. |

### Volume vs. direction (arm 3)

Let `d_rand = NDS(R20_s0) - NDS(R7b_s0)` and `d_conj = -8.18`.

| Outcome | What the paper will say |
|---|---|
| **B1** `d_rand` CI includes zero | Removing 61.6% of the pseudo-labels at random costs nothing measurable; the conjunctive arm's loss is attributable to *which* boxes the camera vetoes, not how many. The strongest available support for the mechanism claim. |
| **B2** `d_rand` negative but `NDS(R19_s0) - NDS(R20_s0)` CI excludes zero | Both volume and direction contribute. We report the decomposition with both intervals and attribute only the residual to the camera veto. §4.4's "the damage is confined to exactly the classes the gate can act on" survives as the mechanism, with its magnitude reduced. |
| **B3** `NDS(R19_s0) - NDS(R20_s0)` CI includes zero | **The 8.18 NDS is a volume effect, not a camera-direction effect.** The mechanism claim in §4.4 and §5 is not supported and will be withdrawn. The paper would then report that the conjunctive arm is uninformative about camera evidence, and the confidence-gate confound is only partly closed. We commit to reporting this outcome as prominently as B1. |

B3 is the outcome that costs us the most and it is the reason this control is
being run. It gets the same abstract-level treatment as B1.

## Exclusion / abort criteria

- Any run whose `epoch_10.pth` is missing, or whose eval does not reach
  `val_full=6019`, is rerun, not reported partially.
- The subset assertion inside `filter_perclass_random_prune` failing aborts the
  build; a non-subset mask would make the matched count meaningless.
- If the pseudo-infos build produces a kept-box count for R20 that differs from
  R19's `241,094`, the arm is not launched until the discrepancy is explained.
- Pickles must be built with `envs/teocc/bin/python` (numpy 1.26.4). A
  numpy-2.x pickle is unreadable by the training env; this has bitten the
  project twice and is recorded in MANIFEST 2026-08-05.

## Appendix — outcome, recorded 2026-08-12 after all three arms completed

Both decision rules resolved. Neither was edited after the runs started.

### Seed replication → **A1**

| seed | base (R7b) | conjunctive (R19) | Δ NDS | paired 95% CI | replicates ≥ 0 | removed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.4150 | 0.3332 | −8.18 | [−9.63, −6.44] | 0/1000 | 61.6% |
| 1 | 0.4609 | 0.3855 | −7.54 | [−10.26, −5.73] | 0/1000 | 51.7% |
| 2 | 0.4535 | 0.4068 | −4.66 | [−9.32, −3.31] | 0/1000 | 52.3% |

All three negative, all three CIs exclude zero → **A1**: the veto cost replicates
on three seeds. The seed-0 caveat comes out of limitation (vii), the abstract and
§4.4.

Not anticipated by the rule and reported anyway: the magnitude is strongly
seed-dependent. Seeds 1 and 2 remove almost the same share (51.7% vs 52.3%) and
cost −7.54 vs −4.66. Volume alone does not explain the spread across seeds.

### Volume vs direction → **B2**

| contrast | point | paired 95% CI | replicates ≥ 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| R20 − R7b — pure volume | **−7.70** | [−8.77, −3.92] | 0/1000 |
| R19 − R20 — direction | **−0.48** | [−4.10, −0.05] | 16/1000 |

The direction interval excludes zero, so **B2**, not B3. Applied as written: both
contribute, and only the −0.48 residual is attributed to the camera veto.

Three qualifications, all of which go in the paper:

1. **Volume accounts for 94% of the conjunctive loss** by point estimate.
2. **The exclusion is marginal** — upper limit −0.05, 16/1000 replicates ≥ 0.
   The interval is 4 NDS wide because R20's own scene-level SE is 0.0157,
   double R19's 0.0081 and R7b's 0.0078: randomising *which* supervision is
   removed makes the student markedly more sensitive to which scenes it is
   evaluated on. R7d, the row-shuffle intervention, shows the same signature
   (SE 0.0152), so this is a property of randomised supervision rather than an
   artifact of this one draw.
3. **Single draw.** As pre-registered, this interval covers evaluation-set
   uncertainty only, not draw-to-draw variance of the random pruning itself.

### Outside both rules — an argument that does not survive

§4.4's mechanism argument rests on the passthrough classes as an "internal
control group": camera support cannot veto `barrier` or `traffic_cone`, and
those are the only two classes that do not lose AP, so "the damage is confined
to exactly the classes the gate can act on".

**The volume-matched control reproduces that table.** Because matching is
per (keyframe, class) and R19 removes nothing from the passthrough classes, the
random control removes nothing from them either — so they cannot move, whatever
the mechanism. Measured on seed 0, ΔAP against the base:

| | in-vocabulary mean (8 classes) | passthrough mean (2 classes) |
|---|---|---|
| camera veto (R19) | −0.121 | +0.006 |
| random pruning (R20) | −0.102 | +0.008 |

`bicycle` and `construction_vehicle` collapse to AP = 0 under both. The
passthrough classes were never a control for the camera treatment; they are a
control for *removal*, and nothing was removed from them. The argument is
withdrawn. What survives is the small, consistently-signed residual — the camera
veto is worse than chance on nearly every in-vocabulary class — which is the
same −0.48 NDS effect seen above, and it is reported at that strength.
